The Serendipitous Expedition

A field notes column · reading time 4 min

The meticulous naturalist beheld a glistening panorama at dawn: an ephemeral mist drifting over the verdant valley, and every quixotic impulse of the indefatigable traveler seemed serendipitous at once.

With meticulous tenacity she traversed the ebullient wilderness, recording each resilient archetype of the local flora in a bulwark of notebooks — a panoramic, almost effervescent inventory of an untrammeled landscape.

By dusk the ephemeral light had faded, yet the indefatigable observer remained: serendipity, she wrote, favors the meticulous.
